## Validation

- Each schema states expected scale and index strategy.
- Permission and records decisions require named approval.
- Examples use synthetic data and contain no client URLs.

## Definition of done

Another developer can design and validate a list, library, or page without creating hidden integration, permission, scale, or records risks.
