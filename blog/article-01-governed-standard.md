# Why Low-Code Teams Need a Governed AI-Assisted Standard

Low-code tools make it quick to create an app or workflow. A team can still get inconsistent results if each person invents a different intake, review, testing, and handoff process. Adding AI to isolated prompting does not solve the coordination problem; it makes clear boundaries and repeatable checks more important.

In a fictional training-request example, a team starts by defining the user need, required fields, human decisions, and acceptance criteria. A reusable delivery standard then describes which work is reviewed by a person, what can be checked deterministically, and what evidence is retained. AI may help draft or inspect an artifact, but a named human role remains accountable for design and release decisions.

The example is a process illustration, not a report of a deployed project or measured improvement. It intentionally excludes real user records, tenant information, internal architecture, and pilot history.

**Limitation:** A process diagram cannot establish that a standard improved quality or delivery outcomes; that requires approved, comparable evidence.
