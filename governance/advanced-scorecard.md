# Advanced Rating Scorecard

Use this as the proposed measurable interpretation of the Q4 rating. The manager-approved corporate rubric remains authoritative.

| Dimension | Developing | Competent | Advanced target | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Partial practices | Complete standard used once | Versioned standard applied to both pilots | Release history, pilot evidence |
| Reusable patterns | Isolated prompts | Core templates and skills | At least 3 skills used by at least 2 developers | Skill registry and run logs |
| Efficiency | Anecdotal | Baseline captured | At least one repeatable activity shows >=25% median cycle-time improvement | Baseline/outcome data |
| Quality | Informal review | Defined QA process | No open Sev 1/2 at release; >=95% critical regression pass | Test reports |
| Critical coverage | Incomplete | Key paths tested | 100% destructive, security, and records-sensitive paths covered | Traceability matrix |
| Adoption | Author-only | Team trained | Another developer completes an accepted change independently | Challenge evidence |
| Training | Ad hoc | Training delivered | >=80% of target team completes training; 3 practical passes | Attendance and assessments |
| Governance | Some controls | Controls documented | Every pilot has security, records, licensing, rollback, support, and owner decisions | Gate checklist |
| Handoff | Documents exist | Successor walkthrough | Successor executes regression and support tasks without author intervention | Observation record |
| Leadership | Work demonstrated | Results summarized | Leadership accepts portfolio, ownership, risk state, and next backlog | Sign-off |

## Measurement rules

- Record the baseline before introducing the skill or automation.
- Compare like-for-like work using the same start and stop definitions.
- Report sample size, median, range, exclusions, and data source.
- Separate AI generation time, human review time, rework, and waiting time.
- Never represent projected savings as realized savings.
