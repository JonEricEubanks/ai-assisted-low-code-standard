# Q4 Goal Charter

## Goal

By December 31, 2026, design, pilot, and operationalize a governed AI-assisted low-code delivery system for Power Apps, Power Automate, SharePoint lists, modern pages, document libraries, and related Microsoft 365 and Power Platform components. Prove the system on ELM and Glencoe Early Check, enable at least one developer other than the author to reuse it successfully, and retain auditable evidence of improved quality, efficiency, security, handoff readiness, and team adoption.

## Business outcome

Appropriately scoped solutions can be delivered consistently within a normal quarterly client project cycle without depending on one developer's undocumented knowledge.

## In scope

- Intake, discovery, architecture, implementation, QA, release, handoff, and retrospective
- Power Apps Canvas apps
- Power Automate cloud flows
- SharePoint lists, libraries, and modern pages
- Reusable prompts, skills, agent instructions, templates, and patterns
- Deterministic, visual, exploratory, and human validation
- ALM, security, accessibility, records, licensing, support, and evidence
- Team training and independent reuse

## Out of scope for Q4

- Autonomous production deployment or security changes
- Replacement of platform-native validation
- Full automation of every Microsoft 365 surface
- Claims of efficiency without a measured baseline
- Publishing client-sensitive implementation details

## Non-negotiables

1. One accountable human owner for every solution and release.
2. Requirements and impact analysis precede implementation.
3. Unknown facts are logged, not inferred.
4. Least privilege and approved data boundaries apply.
5. Deterministic checks and human approval remain release authorities.
6. Records, licensing, rollback, and support decisions are documented.
7. Every pilot retains traceable evidence.
8. A successor must demonstrate independent use.

## Stage gates

| Gate | Exit criteria |
|---|---|
| G0 Charter | Goal, owners, pilots, metrics, evidence location, and rubric approved |
| G1 Standard design | Version 1 standards, templates, controls, and first three skills approved |
| G2 Validation ready | Test identities, synthetic data, evidence schema, deterministic runner, and visual process ready |
| G3 ELM pilot | ELM regression pack, runbook, evidence, and successor walkthrough accepted |
| G4 Early Check pilot | Integration, records, approval, failure, and regression evidence accepted |
| G5 Adoption | Training delivered and independent developer challenge completed |
| G6 Advanced close | Metrics, portfolio, leadership demo, risks, ownership, and Q1 backlog accepted |

## Decisions requiring manager confirmation

- Final Advanced rating thresholds
- Named sponsor and control approvers
- Target developers and training population
- Approved evidence repository
- Approved Foundry and computer-use boundary
- Whether the 25% cycle-time target is an official commitment or an evidence goal
