# SharePoint Delivery

## Procedure

1. Identify whether each asset is a list, library, page, content type, view, or supporting configuration.
2. Define schema, stable internal names, relationships, indexes, volume, views, versioning, and ownership.
3. Define permissions and inheritance; minimize unique permissions.
4. Record retention, records, sensitivity, approval, search, and archive decisions.
5. Map all apps, flows, forms, pages, and connectors that consume the asset.
6. Create an approved provisioning/change plan.
7. Validate schema, permissions, scale, integrations, accessibility, responsive behavior, recovery, and rollback.
8. Update the data dictionary, inventory, runbook, and evidence.

## Stop conditions

Do not infer retention, delete content, break inheritance, or alter production schema without the appropriate human approval.
