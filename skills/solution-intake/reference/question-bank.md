# Clarification Question Bank

Questions are tiered by consequence, not by topic.

| Tier | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Blocking. The answer changes the architecture, cost, or legal position. | Cannot mark Ready |
| Tier 2 | Shaping. The answer changes design or effort but not direction. | Can proceed with a recorded assumption |
| Tier 3 | Refining. The answer affects polish or sequencing. | Resolve during build |

## Discipline rule

Send no more than **eight Tier 1 questions** in one round. Long question lists produce no answers. Ask what blocks the build, get answers, then ask the next round.

Route every question to a role, not to "the client." Unrouted questions do not get answered.

---

## Tier 1 — Ownership and authority

1. Who is the named business owner accountable for this solution after go-live, and who is their backup?
2. Who has authority to approve scope changes and sign off on release?
3. When two departments disagree about process, who decides?
4. Who is the first point of contact when something breaks?

## Tier 1 — Records and retention

5. Is the output of this process an official record?
6. If yes, which system is the system of record, and what retention schedule applies?
7. Does this content fall under public-records, FOIA, legal-hold, or eDiscovery obligations?
8. If the content is also stored elsewhere, which copy is authoritative?

Ask these on any request touching enforcement, adjudication, finance, personnel, permits, or inspections — regardless of what the constraints field says.

## Tier 1 — Access and data

9. Who may view each category of data, and who may edit it?
10. Will anyone outside the organization need access?
11. Does this contain personal, financial, health, or employment-sensitive data?
12. Which identity groups already exist that we can use rather than creating new ones?

## Tier 1 — Scope boundary

13. What is explicitly out of scope for this phase?
14. Is there a date this must be operational by, and what happens if it is not?
15. Is this replacing an existing system, running alongside it, or new?
16. Does historical data need to come forward, and in what volume?

## Tier 1 — Licensing

17. Does any required capability depend on a premium connector or paid license?
18. Who approves recurring license cost, and is that approval already in place?

---

## Tier 2 — Process

19. What are the stages a case moves through from creation to closure?
20. What triggers movement between stages, and who performs it?
21. What are the exception paths — rejection, reassignment, cancellation, reopening?
22. What happens when a deadline passes with no action?
23. Can the same subject have more than one case open at once?

## Tier 2 — Field work and devices

24. What devices will be used, and are they organization-managed?
25. Is connectivity reliable at the point of work?
26. If connectivity fails mid-task, what must happen to work in progress?
27. Is the work ever performed by someone who is not a licensed internal user?

## Tier 2 — Notifications

28. Who must be notified, on what event, and through which channel?
29. Is the notification informational, or does it require an action and a response?
30. What is the escalation path when nobody responds?
31. Is a digest acceptable, or is each event individually significant?

## Tier 2 — Media and documents

32. Are photos or documents required evidence, or supporting context?
33. Are they ever mandatory before a step can complete?
34. What file sizes and quantities are expected per case?
35. Must any document be generated for signature, mailing, or filing?

## Tier 2 — Volume

36. How many records exist today, and how many are added per year?
37. How many concurrent users at peak?
38. How long must records remain actively accessible before archive?

---

## Tier 3 — Experience and reporting

39. What must a user see first when they open the solution?
40. Which views or filters matter most day to day?
41. What does leadership need to see, how often, and in what format?
42. Are there accessibility requirements beyond organizational baseline?
43. What naming and terminology does the client use that we should mirror?

---

## Question routing

| Topic | Route to |
|---|---|
| Ownership, approval, scope | Business owner and SDM |
| Records, retention, legal | Records officer, clerk, or legal |
| Access, identity, security | IT or security owner |
| Licensing and cost | IT and account owner |
| Process and exceptions | Operational subject-matter expert |
| Devices and connectivity | IT and field supervisor |
| Volume | Operational supervisor |

## Recording answers

Every answer is recorded with source, date, and the person who provided it. An unattributed answer is an assumption wearing a disguise.
