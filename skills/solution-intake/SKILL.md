# Solution Intake

## Inputs

Business request, sponsor, users, desired outcome, deadline, known systems, and available evidence.

## Procedure

1. Create an intake brief from `templates/solution-intake.md`.
2. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, unknowns, and recommendations.
3. Identify personas, business journeys, data, integrations, volume, accessibility, security, records, licensing, support, and timing.
4. Classify solution complexity and risk.
5. Write measurable acceptance criteria.
6. Identify the accountable solution owner and required approvers.
7. Decide whether the request is ready for architecture, needs discovery, or must be re-scoped.

## Stop conditions

Stop and request clarification when there is no accountable owner, no testable outcome, sensitive data has no approved boundary, or production change is requested without normal approval.

## Output

Completed intake brief, open-question register, initial risks, acceptance criteria, and recommended next skill.
