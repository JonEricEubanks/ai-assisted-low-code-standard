# Power Desk Operational Evidence Matrix

This matrix identifies the evidence needed to operate, improve, and hand off the Power Desk delivery system. It is not an employee portfolio or performance-rating artifact. Synthetic examples illustrate structure only and never represent completed client work.

| Operational area | Evidence required | Example source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard adoption | Versioned standard applied to delivered solutions | Release history and approved solution records |
| Reusable engineering | Approved skills and templates used by multiple developers | Skill registry and usage records |
| Delivery efficiency | Comparable baseline and outcome measurements | Measurement records |
| Quality | Defect status and critical regression results | QA reports |
| Critical coverage | Destructive, security, permission, and records-sensitive paths tested | Traceability matrix |
| Developer onboarding | New or current developer completes a guided delivery task | Onboarding exercise and feedback |
| Governance | Security, records, licensing, rollback, support, and owner decisions recorded | Gate checklists |
| Maintainability | Successor executes regression and support tasks | Handoff acceptance record |
| Continuous improvement | Retrospective findings produce traceable standard revisions | Retrospective and change history |

## Case-study structure

1. Synthetic problem and prior process
2. Assumptions, constraints, and risks
3. Architecture and decisions
4. Standard or skill used
5. Human and AI/tool responsibilities
6. Validation approach and evidence plan
7. Synthetic failure and correction example
8. Metric definitions and evidence status
9. Independent-reuse evidence required
10. Lessons and follow-up

## Claim boundary

Do not state or imply that a process improved measured outcomes, enabled independent reuse, or passed a delivery gate until the relevant evidence has been reviewed and approved. Never present a synthetic scenario as a client result.
