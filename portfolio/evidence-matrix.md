# Senior Developer Portfolio Evidence Matrix

This matrix maps public-safe evidence types to Senior Developer capabilities and all dimensions in the proposed Advanced-rating scorecard. The corporate rubric and manager decision remain authoritative. The examples in this folder are synthetic scaffolding, not pilot evidence or proof that a rating target has been met.

| Senior capability | Proposed scorecard dimension | Target proof / evidence required | Public-safe artifact or evidence source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical leadership | Standard | Versioned standard applied to both pilots; source-cited release and pilot evidence | [Synthetic case studies](synthetic-case-study-a.md), [demo outline](leadership-demo-outline.md); pilot evidence pending |
| Repeatable engineering | Reusable patterns | At least 3 skills used by at least 2 developers; registry and run logs | [Blog series](../blog/blog-series-plan.md); usage evidence pending |
| Business impact | Efficiency | At least one repeatable activity shows at least 25% median cycle-time improvement on comparable, sourced data | [Measurement article](../blog/article-06-measurement.md); no realized result claimed |
| Quality engineering | Quality | No open Sev 1/2 at release and at least 95% critical regression pass | [Synthetic QA visual](synthetic-visuals.md#qa); release evidence pending |
| Quality engineering | Critical coverage | Full destructive, security, and records-sensitive path coverage | [Synthetic QA visual](synthetic-visuals.md#qa); traceability evidence pending |
| Enablement | Adoption | Another developer completes an accepted change independently | [Adoption visual](synthetic-visuals.md#adoption); challenge evidence pending |
| Enablement | Training | At least 80% target-team completion and 3 practical passes | [Adoption visual](synthetic-visuals.md#adoption); attendance and assessment evidence pending |
| Governance | Governance | Security, records, licensing, rollback, support, and owner decisions recorded for every pilot | [Publication review](../blog/publication-review.md) covers public claims only; pilot decisions pending |
| Maintainability | Handoff | Successor executes regression and support tasks without author intervention | [Case-study template](case-study-template.md); successor evidence pending |
| Communication | Leadership | Leadership accepts portfolio, ownership, risk state, and next backlog | [Leadership demo outline](leadership-demo-outline.md); sign-off pending |
| Continuous improvement | Standard and reusable patterns | Pilot lessons lead to traceable standard revisions | [Case-study template](case-study-template.md); approved retrospective evidence pending |

## Case-study structure

1. Synthetic problem and prior process
2. Assumptions, constraints, and risks
3. Architecture and decisions
4. Standard/skill used
5. Human versus AI/tool responsibilities
6. Validation approach and evidence plan
7. Synthetic failure/correction example
8. Metric definitions and evidence status
9. Independent-reuse evidence required
10. Lessons and follow-up

## Claim boundary

Do not state or imply that the standard has been validated on pilots, improved measured outcomes, enabled independent reuse, or met an Advanced target until the relevant evidence has been reviewed and approved for the claim. Never turn a synthetic scenario into a pilot result.
