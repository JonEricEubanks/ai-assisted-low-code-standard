# Senior Developer Portfolio Evidence Matrix

| Senior capability | Evidence to retain | Target proof |
|---|---|---|
| Technical leadership | Standards architecture and decisions | Coherent operating model approved |
| Repeatable engineering | Skills, templates, version history | Used on both pilots |
| Quality engineering | QA strategy and release evidence | Critical thresholds met |
| Governance | Security, records, licensing, ALM decisions | Complete for every pilot |
| Enablement | Training and independent challenge | Another developer succeeds |
| Business impact | Baseline and outcome metrics | Measurable improvement |
| Maintainability | Runbook and successor verification | Successor supports solution |
| Communication | Blog, demo, and leadership presentation | Complex approach explained clearly |
| Continuous improvement | Retrospectives and revisions | Pilot lessons change standard |

## Case-study structure

1. Business problem and prior process
2. Constraints and risks
3. Architecture and decisions
4. Standard/skill used
5. Human versus AI/MCP responsibilities
6. Build and validation evidence
7. Failure or correction
8. Before/after metrics
9. Independent reuse
10. Business outcome and lessons

## Promotion narrative

The strongest claim is not "I built an AI agent." It is:

> I designed and operationalized a governed delivery system that improved consistency and quality, enabled another developer to work independently, and was validated on real client solutions with measurable evidence.
